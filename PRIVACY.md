# Privacy

The extension stores artwork files, file hashes, listing metadata, queue state, published URLs, recorded selectors and activity logs locally in IndexedDB/Chrome extension storage. It uses your existing TeePublic login session without reading or exporting cookies or passwords.

A user-started upload submits the selected artwork and listing to TeePublic. Publication requires explicit batch approval and website-terms consent. No analytics, remote logging, paid API or external backend is included.

Diagnostic exports can contain filenames, listing text and design URLs. Review/redact them before sharing. Do not attach credentials, session cookies, unpublished artwork or account screenshots to public issues.

Delete All Success only hides rows locally and preserves duplicate history. To remove all locally retained data, remove the extension or clear its extension storage; export your results first. This does not delete published TeePublic designs.
